Frequently asked questions

Why is 4 written IV and not IIII?

Subtractive notation places a smaller numeral before a larger one: IV means 5 − 1 = 4. The classics used this for 4, 9, 40, 90, 400 and 900.

What is the largest number supported?

3999 (MMMCMXCIX). The classic system has no symbol for 5000 and above, which is why later systems added an overline.

Will it accept "IIII"?

No. Only canonical numerals are accepted — non-standard forms are rejected with a clear message instead of guessed.

Does it run offline?

Yes. The conversion is pure arithmetic in your browser, with no network calls.
