Text to Speech

Free text to speech in your browser: paste text, pick a voice, speed and pitch, and listen. Runs locally with your device's own voices — nothing is uploaded.

🔒 Your files never leave your device

About Text to Speech

Paste any text and have it read aloud using your browser's built-in voices. Choose the voice, speed and pitch, then listen instantly — everything happens on your device, so nothing is uploaded.

How to use Text to Speech

  1. Paste or type the text to read.
  2. Pick a voice, speed and pitch.
  3. Press Speak; pause or stop anytime.

Supported formats & options

  • Input: any text
  • Output: spoken audio via the browser's speech-synthesis voices

Common use cases

  • Proof-listening to an article or draft
  • Accessibility — reading text aloud when you can't read the screen
  • Language learners hearing words pronounced

Frequently asked questions

Can I record or download the audio?

No — this tool reads aloud only. Browsers can't reliably capture speech-synthesis output, so audio download isn't offered.

Which voices are available?

Your operating system's voices — the set varies by device and browser, and voices can take a moment to load on first use.

Is my text uploaded?

No — speech synthesis runs entirely in your browser.
