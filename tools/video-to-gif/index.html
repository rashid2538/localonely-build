About Video to GIF

Turn any video clip into an animated GIF without uploading it anywhere. Pick the frame rate and output width, watch the conversion progress, and download a GIF rendered frame-by-frame right in your browser.

How to use Video to GIF

  1. Drop a video file.
  2. Choose the FPS (5–24) and output width.
  3. Convert, preview and download the GIF.

Supported formats & options

Common use cases

  • Creating lightweight previews of longer videos
  • Building GIFs for docs, emails and chat without a service

Frequently asked questions

Why does my GIF look blocky?

GIF is limited to 256 colors per frame. Lower FPS and smaller widths compress better; the tool's defaults (10 FPS, 320 px) are a good starting point.

How long can the clip be?

Any length, but longer clips at high FPS produce large files quickly — keep FPS ≤ 15 and width ≤ 480 for the best size.

Is my video uploaded?

No — frames are captured and encoded locally with the gifenc library.
