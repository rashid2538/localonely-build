About Image Compressor

Compress an image to a target file size — 100 KB, 500 KB or anything you set. The tool re-encodes the image in your browser, binary-searching the quality until the output fits, then shows the before/after sizes.

How to use Image Compressor

  1. Choose a JPG, PNG or WebP image.
  2. Pick the target size (≤100 KB, ≤500 KB or custom) and output format.
  3. Compress, compare the before/after sizes, and download.

Supported formats & options

  • Input: JPG / PNG / WebP
  • Output: compressed JPG / PNG / WebP

Common use cases

  • Meeting upload limits on forms, forums and email
  • Fitting images in web templates and CMSs
  • Shrinking photos before messaging or sharing

Frequently asked questions

Is the compressed image uploaded anywhere?

No. Re-encoding happens entirely in your browser — the original never leaves your device.

Why can’t PNG reach the target?

PNG is lossless, so there is no quality to trade for size. Choose JPEG or WebP for real reductions; PNG works best for graphics with flat color.

Will compression reduce quality?

The tool stops at the highest quality that still fits your target, so you get the smallest file that stays under the limit — not a fixed low quality.
