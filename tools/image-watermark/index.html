Image Watermark

Stamp a text watermark on any image — position, rotation, size, opacity and tiling. Free watermark tool that runs entirely in your browser, no uploads.

About Image Watermark

Protect your photos and designs with a text watermark. Place your name or © notice in any of nine positions, rotate it, tune the size and opacity, or tile it across the whole image for stronger protection — all rendered locally.

How to use Image Watermark

  1. Drop an image onto the page.
  2. Type your watermark text and pick position, size, opacity, rotation and color.
  3. Optionally tile the mark across the image, then download.

Supported formats & options

  • Input: PNG, JPEG, WebP, …
  • Output: watermarked PNG, WebP or JPEG

Common use cases

  • Protecting original photography posted online
  • Branding preview images and mockups with a © notice
  • Discouraging casual reuse of your graphics and illustrations

Frequently asked questions

How do I make the watermark hard to remove?

Tile the mark across the whole image and keep opacity above 60% — cropping a corner then cuts through a mark instead of around it.

Can I rotate the watermark?

Yes, from −45° to +45°, which gives the classic diagonal watermark look.

Is my image uploaded?

No — the watermark is drawn with the canvas API in your browser. Nothing leaves your device.
