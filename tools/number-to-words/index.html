Number to Words

Convert numbers to English words instantly — plain or British "and" style, up to 999 trillion, with decimals and negatives. Free, runs 100% in your browser.

About Number to Words

Convert any number into English words — 12345 becomes "twelve thousand three hundred forty five" — with a British-English "and" variant that matches how amounts are spoken on cheques. Handles integers up to 999 trillion, decimals and negative values.

How to use Number to Words

  1. Type the number (or paste it).
  2. Choose plain or British "and" style.
  3. Read the words and copy them for a cheque, invoice or form.

Supported formats & options

  • Input: any number up to 999 trillion
  • Output: the number written in English words

Common use cases

  • Writing cheque amounts in words
  • Spelling out amounts on invoices and legal documents
  • Learning and teaching how large numbers are read

Frequently asked questions

What is the British "and" style?

It inserts "and" the way amounts are spoken: 230 → "two hundred and thirty", 1001 → "one thousand and one".

Does it handle decimals?

Yes — 12.5 becomes "twelve point five", reading each decimal digit separately like spoken numbers.

What is the largest number supported?

Up to 999,999,999,999,999 (999 trillion). Beyond that the tool asks you to enter a smaller number.

Is my number uploaded?

No — conversion is pure arithmetic in your browser.
