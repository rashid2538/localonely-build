About Time Zone Converter

Convert any date and time between two real IANA time zones — DST is handled automatically, so you never have to remember whether London is ahead or behind today.

How to use Time Zone Converter

  1. Pick the date and time you want to convert.
  2. Choose the source and target time zones.
  3. Read the converted time and offset, and scan the world-clock grid.

Supported formats & options

  • Input: local date/time + two IANA zones
  • Output: converted time, UTC equivalent, world-clock grid

Common use cases

  • Scheduling meetings across time zones
  • Converting travel or flight times
  • Coordinating with remote teams and clients abroad

Frequently asked questions

Does it handle daylight saving time?

Yes — conversions use the browser's IANA database, so summer/winter offsets apply automatically.

How do I convert a specific future date?

Set the date and time in the first field; the grid and target update for that exact instant.

What zones are supported?

All IANA zones the browser knows, with a curated list of common ones in the dropdowns.
