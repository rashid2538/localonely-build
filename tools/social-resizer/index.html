About Social Media Resizer

Resize an image to the exact dimensions each platform expects — 1200×630 Open Graph, 1080×1080 Instagram post, 1080×1920 story, 851×315 Facebook cover, 1280×720 YouTube thumbnail and more. Non-matching aspect ratios are center-cropped to fill the target frame.

How to use Social Media Resizer

  1. Drop in the image you want to resize.
  2. Pick a platform preset (or generate all eight at once).
  3. Choose the output format and quality, then download.

Supported formats & options

  • Input: any image
  • Output: resized images at platform preset sizes

Common use cases

  • Creating shareable link-preview images for a website
  • Preparing Instagram posts and stories at the right ratio
  • Making YouTube thumbnails and LinkedIn banners

Frequently asked questions

What sizes are included?

Open Graph 1200×630, Twitter card 1200×675, Instagram 1080×1080, Instagram story 1080×1920, Facebook cover 851×315, YouTube 1280×720, Pinterest 1000×1500 and LinkedIn banner 1584×396.

How are mismatched aspect ratios handled?

The image is scaled to cover the target frame and center-cropped — nothing is stretched or distorted.

Is my image uploaded?

No — resizing happens on a canvas in your browser.
