About Days Between Dates

Find the exact span between any two dates — total days, weeks, business days and a full years/months/days breakdown — or count down to a target date. All arithmetic runs on UTC, so daylight-saving changes never skew the result.

How to use Days Between Dates

  1. Pick a From and a To date.
  2. Read the total days, business days, weeks and calendar breakdown.
  3. Optional: set a target date for a countdown from today.

Supported formats & options

  • Input: two dates (or one target date for the countdown)
  • Output: total days, business days, total weeks, years/months/days

Common use cases

  • Project and deadline planning
  • Contract and notice-period counting
  • School-term and event countdowns

Frequently asked questions

Are the end dates included?

The span counts full 24-hour days between the two dates; business days count weekdays in that same span.

How is business days counted?

Weekdays Monday–Friday between the two dates, excluding weekends. It does not know about holidays — you can subtract those yourself.

Why UTC?

Some time zones have 23- and 25-hour days during DST changes. By counting in UTC, the tool guarantees every result is a whole number.

Can I count down to a birthday or deadline?

Yes — the countdown section shows days, business days and the calendar breakdown until any target date.
