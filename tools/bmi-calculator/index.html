Frequently asked questions

What is a healthy BMI?

The WHO defines 18.5–24.9 kg/m² as normal for adults. Below 18.5 is underweight; 25–29.9 is overweight; 30 and above is obese.

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

BMI is a screening tool, not a diagnosis — it doesn't account for muscle mass, age or body composition, so muscular athletes can read as overweight.

Are my measurements uploaded?

No — everything is computed locally in your browser.
