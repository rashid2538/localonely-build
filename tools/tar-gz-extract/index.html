TAR.GZ Extractor

Extract .tar.gz, .tgz and plain TAR archives in your browser — list contents, select files, download them. Free archive extractor, no upload, no install.

🔒 Your files never leave your device

About TAR.GZ Extractor

Open a .tar.gz, .tgz or plain TAR archive and extract the files you need — no compression tool required. The archive is decompressed and parsed entirely in your browser, with nested paths preserved and filenames sanitized so nothing escapes the download folder.

How to use TAR.GZ Extractor

  1. Drop a .tar.gz, .tgz or .tar file.
  2. Review the extracted file list with sizes.
  3. Select the files you want and download them individually or together.

Supported formats & options

  • Input: .tar.gz / .tgz / .tar archive
  • Output: extracted files, downloaded individually or as a batch

Common use cases

  • Opening tarballs without a terminal or archive app
  • Pulling a few files out of a large archive
  • Extracting downloaded source tarballs on any device

Frequently asked questions

Does it handle long filenames?

Yes — GNU long-name entries are resolved, and the modern gzip layer is decompressed with the browser's built-in DecompressionStream.

Can it open a plain .tar without gzip?

Yes — if the file is not gzip-compressed, it is parsed directly as a TAR archive.

Is my archive uploaded?

No — decompression and parsing happen entirely in your browser.
