Frequently asked questions

What is the median?

The middle value when the list is sorted — the average of the two middle values for an even-sized list.

What if there is no mode?

When every value appears exactly once, the mode is shown as "—".

Are my numbers stored?

No — everything is computed in your browser and nothing is saved or uploaded.
