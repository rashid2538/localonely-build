Lottery Numbers

Generate lottery numbers for Powerball, Mega Millions, EuroMillions, Lotto 6/49 and more, with the real draw rules. Free, CSPRNG-fair and runs in your browser.

🔒 Your files never leave your device

About Lottery Numbers

Draw random lottery tickets that follow each game's real rules — five mains plus a Powerball, five plus a Mega Ball, two EuroMillions Lucky Stars, or a straight 6/49 line. Balls come from the browser's cryptographically secure random source and are deduplicated, so no ticket repeats.

How to use Lottery Numbers

  1. Pick a lottery format — Powerball, Mega Millions, EuroMillions, Lotto 6/49, Pick 3 or Pick 4.
  2. Choose how many tickets to draw.
  3. Press Draw and copy the numbers.

Supported formats & options

  • Input: lottery format + ticket count
  • Output: drawn main numbers and bonus balls per ticket

Common use cases

  • Generating quick-pick tickets for Powerball or Mega Millions
  • Filling a 6/49 or EuroMillions slip without picking numbers
  • Random team or classroom draws with a dice-roll feel

Frequently asked questions

Are the numbers really random?

Yes — they come from the browser's Cryptographically Secure PRNG (the same source used for encryption keys), so every combination has equal odds.

Can the same number appear twice on a ticket?

No. Mains and bonus pools draw unique balls per ticket, and duplicate tickets are filtered out automatically.

Does this improve my chances of winning?

No — every combination has identical odds. The tool just saves you the effort of choosing.

Are my numbers stored?

No. Everything is generated locally in your browser and nothing is saved or uploaded.
