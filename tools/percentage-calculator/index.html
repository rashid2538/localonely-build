Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Choose "What is p% of x?" and enter the number and the percent. The result is x × p ÷ 100 — the tool shows each step.

How do I find the original price before a discount?

Choose the "Original before a p% change" mode, switch to "Decreased by p%", and enter the discounted price and the discount percent.

Does the calculator store my numbers?

No — everything is computed in your browser and nothing is saved or uploaded.
