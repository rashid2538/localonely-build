About Discount Calculator

Calculate the sale price and savings for any discount, apply stacked discounts (like "30% off, then an extra 10%") in order, or reverse-engineer an original price from a discounted one — all with the working shown.

How to use Discount Calculator

  1. Pick a mode: price & % off, stacked discounts, or reverse.
  2. Enter the price and discount percentage (or a comma-separated stack).
  3. Read the sale price, savings and running totals instantly.

Supported formats & options

  • Input: price and discount % (or a stack)
  • Output: sale price, savings, running totals

Common use cases

  • Checking deals while shopping
  • Pricing products with a margin-off strategy
  • Figuring out what an item cost before a sale

Frequently asked questions

How do stacked discounts work?

Each % is applied to the running total, so "30% then 10% off $120" is $120 → $84 → $75.60 — not 40% off. The tool shows every step.

How do I find the original price before a discount?

Switch to the reverse mode and enter the sale price plus the discount % — the original price and the amount saved are calculated.

Is there a limit on stack length?

No — enter any number of comma- or plus-separated percentages, applied left to right.
