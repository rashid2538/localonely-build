About Work Hours Calculator

Add your shift start and end times plus lunch breaks, and get per-day hours, the weekly total in decimal and h:mm form, and a daily average — night shifts included.

How to use Work Hours Calculator

  1. Enter each day's start, end and break minutes.
  2. Add or remove rows to match your week.
  3. Read the totals — decimal hours and h:mm.

Supported formats & options

  • Input: per-day start/end times + break minutes
  • Output: per-day hours, weekly total, daily average

Common use cases

  • Filling in timesheets and payroll forms
  • Tracking hours across multiple part-time jobs
  • Working out overtime or week totals

Frequently asked questions

What time formats can I use?

Both 24-hour (9:00, 17:30) and 12-hour (9am, 5:30 PM) are accepted.

How are night shifts handled?

An end time earlier than the start is treated as wrapping past midnight (e.g. 22:00 to 06:00 = 8 hours).

Are breaks subtracted automatically?

Yes — enter the break in minutes per day and it is deducted from that day's hours.
