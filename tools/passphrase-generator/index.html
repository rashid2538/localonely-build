About Passphrase Generator

Create passphrases that are both memorable and strong: several random words joined by a separator, drawn uniformly from a 470-word list by Web Crypto. The entropy readout shows exactly how hard it is to guess.

How to use Passphrase Generator

  1. Pick how many words, the separator and whether to append a digit.
  2. Optionally capitalize each word for extra visual distinction.
  3. Click Generate, then copy the ones you like or download them.

Supported formats & options

  • Input: word count, separator, digit & capitalization options
  • Output: 1–10 passphrases with an entropy readout

Common use cases

  • Replacing weak passwords with something you can actually remember
  • Securing master passwords and recovery keys
  • Teaching others good passphrase hygiene

Frequently asked questions

Why words instead of characters?

A few random words are far easier to type and remember than random characters, and carry comparable entropy.

How strong is a passphrase?

The page shows bits of entropy: about 8.9 bits per word from the 470-word list, so six words plus a digit ≈ 56 bits.

Are these truly random?

Yes — words are drawn uniformly via Web Crypto (CSPRNG) with rejection sampling, locally in your browser.
