About GPA Calculator

Work out your grade point average course by course. The calculator uses the standard 4.0 scale with plus/minus modifiers, weights each grade by its credit hours, and can add the +1.0 boost for AP, IB and honors courses.

How to use GPA Calculator

  1. Add a row for each course.
  2. Pick the letter grade and enter the credit hours.
  3. Tick "AP / IB / honors" for weighted courses.
  4. Read your GPA, total points and credits.

Supported formats & options

  • Input: letter grade, credit hours, optional weighted flag per course
  • Output: GPA, total grade points, total credits

Common use cases

  • Semester and cumulative GPA checks
  • Deciding whether honors weighting changes your average
  • Comparing course loads across terms

Frequently asked questions

What grade scale is used?

The standard 4.0 scale: A=4.0 down to F=0.0, with +/− modifiers (A- = 3.7, B+ = 3.3, and so on).

How does weighted GPA work?

Tick "weighted" on a course to add +1.0 to that course’s grade points — the typical treatment for AP, IB and honors classes.

Are credits really necessary?

Yes for accuracy. A 4-credit A counts for more than a 1-credit A; the average is weighted by credit hours, which is how real transcripts are computed.

Does it store my grades?

No. Everything is computed locally — refresh the page and it is gone.
