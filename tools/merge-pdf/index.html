Merge PDF

Combine multiple PDF files into one document in seconds. Free online PDF merger that runs 100% in your browser — no uploads, no signup, no file-size limits.

🔒 Your files never leave your device

About Merge PDF

Merge several PDF documents into a single file, in exactly the order you choose. Because everything runs locally in your browser, your documents never leave your device.

How to use Merge PDF

  1. Add the PDF files you want to combine (you can reorder them afterwards).
  2. Drag the files into your preferred order — the preview reflects the final document.
  3. Click Merge to produce one combined PDF, then download it.

Supported formats & options

  • Input: PDF
  • Output: one combined PDF

Common use cases

  • Combining scanned pages and digital PDFs into one document
  • Assembling a single deliverable from multiple report sections
  • Joining e-invoices or statements before sharing

Frequently asked questions

Are my PDFs uploaded anywhere?

No. The merge happens entirely in your browser with the PDF-lib engine — no file ever leaves your device.

Is there a file-size limit?

No artificial limit; only what your browser can handle in memory.

Can I reorder pages after merging?

Yes — reorder the files before merging, or use the Organize PDF tool afterwards for page-level control.
