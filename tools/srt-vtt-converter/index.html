About SRT ↔ VTT Converter

Convert subtitle files between SRT and WebVTT, nudge every cue forward or backward in time, and get clean, valid output with cues sorted and timestamps clamped. Captions are processed locally — they never leave your device.

How to use SRT ↔ VTT Converter

  1. Pick a direction, or leave it on auto-detect.
  2. Upload a .srt or .vtt file, or paste the subtitle text.
  3. Optionally shift cues by a number of seconds, then convert and download.

Supported formats & options

  • Input: .srt or .vtt text or file
  • Output: converted .srt or .vtt (copy or download)

Common use cases

  • Preparing captions for platforms that require WebVTT
  • Converting subtitle files for video editors that expect SRT
  • Fixing a subtitle track that is consistently early or late

Frequently asked questions

How do I fix subtitles that are out of sync?

Enter the offset in the Shift field — positive to make cues appear later, negative to make them earlier — and convert again.

Does it keep cue text and styling?

Cue text, ordering and durations are preserved. WebVTT-only settings like alignment are dropped when converting to SRT, which does not support them.

Is my caption file uploaded?

No — conversion happens entirely in your browser.
