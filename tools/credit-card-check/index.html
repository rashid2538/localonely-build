About Credit Card Validator

Frequently asked questions

Does this check if the card has money?

No. This only verifies the number is structurally valid — it cannot know whether an account exists or has funds, and it never contacts anything.

What is the Luhn algorithm?

A simple checksum: every second digit from the right is doubled (subtracting 9 if over 9) and the total must end in 0. It catches almost all typos.

Are test numbers available?

Yes — the page includes ready-made example numbers for Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover, JCB and Diners that pass validation.

Is my card number uploaded?

No. The number is processed entirely in your browser and never transmitted.
