Audio Trimmer

Trim audio files by time range and export as WAV or MP3, entirely in your browser. Preview before you download — no uploads, no signup.

🔒 Your files never leave your device

About Audio Trimmer

Cut any audio file down to the part you want. Set the start and end times, preview the original and the trimmed clip, then export as lossless WAV or 192 kbps MP3 — all processed locally on your device.

How to use Audio Trimmer

  1. Drop an audio file — WAV, MP3, OGG, M4A or FLAC.
  2. Set the start and end times with the sliders.
  3. Preview the trimmed clip, then export as WAV or MP3.

Supported formats & options

  • Input: WAV/MP3/OGG/M4A/FLAC audio
  • Output: trimmed WAV (lossless) or MP3 (192 kbps)

Common use cases

  • Cutting a ringtone or notification sound
  • Extracting the best clip from a recording
  • Removing silence or preamble from the start of a file

Frequently asked questions

What formats can I trim?

WAV files are parsed by a pure-JS reader; other formats decode through the Web Audio API. Output is WAV or MP3.

Is the MP3 encoding any good?

It uses a pure-JS MP3 encoder at 192 kbps — a solid quality/size trade-off for speech and music.

Can I hear the result before downloading?

Yes — the trimmed clip is playable right on the page before you export.

Is my audio uploaded?

No — everything runs locally in your browser.
