Compare Images

Compare two images side by side with a draggable slider and a pixel-level difference report. Free image comparison tool that runs entirely in your browser.

🔒 Your files never leave your device

About Compare Images

Load two images and wipe between them with a draggable slider to spot differences visually, while a pixel-level report tells you exactly how much changed — the percentage of changed pixels and the average difference.

How to use Compare Images

  1. Choose the first image (before) and the second image (after).
  2. Drag the slider — or press ← → — to wipe between them.
  3. Read the change report: changed pixels, mean absolute difference and a plain-language verdict.

Supported formats & options

  • Input: two images (PNG, JPEG, WebP, …)
  • Output: draggable comparison + pixel-diff statistics

Common use cases

  • Verifying that an edit produced exactly the intended change
  • Spotting compression or re-encoding artifacts
  • Checking that a design or screenshot matches a reference

Frequently asked questions

What does “pixels changed” measure?

Every pixel in the overlapping area is compared; the report shows what percentage differ, and the average difference per channel (0–255) as a severity gauge.

Do the images need the same size?

No — the comparison covers the overlapping area and each image is shown fitted to the stage.

Is my image uploaded?

No — comparison runs locally on your device.
