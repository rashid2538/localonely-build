Frequently asked questions

What exactly is a prime number?

A whole number greater than 1 with exactly two divisors: itself and 1. The first primes are 2, 3, 5, 7, 11 and 13.

Is 1 a prime number?

No. By definition a prime must have exactly two distinct divisors, so 1 is neither prime nor composite.

How fast is the primality check?

Trial division only runs up to the square root, and only over odd numbers — instant for everyday ranges.

Does it handle very large numbers?

Any JavaScript-safe integer. Beyond 9 quadrillion you may see rounding, so numbers that large are best checked with specialized tools.
