Frequently asked questions

What if the discriminant is negative?

The equation has no real roots. The solver returns the two complex conjugate roots using an imaginary term.

What does the discriminant tell me?

Δ = b² − 4ac. Positive means two distinct real roots, zero means one repeated root, negative means two complex roots.

Can a be zero?

No — with a = 0 the equation becomes linear, not quadratic. The solver flags that case instead of mislabeling it.

How is the vertex found?

The axis of symmetry is x = −b/2a; the vertex is the point (x, y) where the parabola peaks or dips.
