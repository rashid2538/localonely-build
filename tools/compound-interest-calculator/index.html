About Compound Interest Calculator

Project the future value of savings or investments with compound interest. Set the starting amount, annual rate, years and compounding frequency, add a monthly contribution, and see the future value, total you put in and interest earned.

How to use Compound Interest Calculator

  1. Enter the starting amount, annual rate and number of years.
  2. Pick how often interest compounds (monthly, quarterly, yearly…) and add a monthly contribution if any.
  3. Read the future value and the interest earned on top of your contributions.

Supported formats & options

  • Input: principal, rate %, years, frequency, contribution
  • Output: future value, contributions, interest earned

Common use cases

  • Projecting retirement or savings growth
  • Comparing compounding frequencies for the same rate
  • Seeing the impact of adding a monthly contribution

Frequently asked questions

What difference does compounding frequency make?

More frequent compounding earns slightly more for the same annual rate because interest is added to the balance more often. The tool lets you compare any frequency.

When is the monthly contribution added?

At the end of each period, which is the standard assumption for savings plans. Contributions grow with the same rate from that point on.

Is my financial data stored?

No — everything is calculated locally in your browser.
