About Loan / EMI Calculator

Work out the monthly EMI on any loan — home, car or personal — with a full amortization schedule. The reducing-balance formula gives you the exact monthly payment, total interest and what portion of each payment goes to principal.

How to use Loan / EMI Calculator

  1. Enter the loan amount, annual interest rate and tenure in months.
  2. Read your monthly EMI, total payment and total interest.
  3. Expand the schedule to see each month’s principal, interest and remaining balance.

Supported formats & options

  • Input: principal, annual rate %, tenure (months)
  • Output: EMI, totals + full amortization schedule

Common use cases

  • Comparing loan offers before borrowing
  • Planning a home or car purchase budget
  • Seeing how much interest you really pay over the term

Frequently asked questions

What is EMI?

Equated Monthly Installment — the fixed amount you pay each month, made up of an interest portion and a principal portion. It uses the reducing-balance formula.

How is the amortization schedule calculated?

Each month, interest is charged on the remaining balance, the rest of the EMI pays down principal, and the balance shrinks. The final row settles any cent-level residual.

Are my loan details stored?

No — every calculation happens locally in your browser.
