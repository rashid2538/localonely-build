About Business Days Calculator

Work with business days — the weekdays between two dates, a date plus or minus a number of working days, or the nearest business day to any date. Weekends are skipped automatically, and all math runs on UTC so daylight-saving changes never skew a count.

How to use Business Days Calculator

  1. Pick a mode: days between, add/subtract business days, or nearest business day.
  2. Enter the dates and/or the number of days.
  3. Read the result — including the weekday name of the resulting date.

Supported formats & options

  • Input: dates and business-day offsets
  • Output: business-day counts and resulting dates

Common use cases

  • Quoting delivery dates as "10 business days"
  • Counting working days for contracts and notice periods
  • Planning around a weekend by finding the next Monday

Frequently asked questions

Are holidays counted as business days?

Only weekends are skipped. There is no reliable offline holiday calendar, so public holidays are counted as business days — subtract them yourself if needed.

How is the count between dates defined?

It counts Monday–Friday in the span from the start date up to (but not including) the end date — the standard convention for deadlines.

Does DST affect the results?

No. All arithmetic runs on UTC dates, so a 23- or 25-hour day never changes the count.

Is my data uploaded?

No — everything is computed locally in your browser.
