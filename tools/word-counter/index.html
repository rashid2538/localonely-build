Free online word counter: words, characters, sentences, paragraphs and estimated reading time. Works fully in your browser — paste text and count instantly.

About Word Counter

Count words, characters, sentences and paragraphs in any text, and get a reading-time estimate. Paste or type and the counts update live — your text stays on your device.

How to use Word Counter

  1. Paste or type your text into the box.
  2. Watch the live counts update as you type.
  3. Copy the result or keep editing — nothing is stored.

Supported formats & options

  • Input: plain text
  • Output: live statistics

Common use cases

  • Meeting essay, article or social-post word limits
  • Estimating reading time for blog posts
  • Checking character limits for forms and bios

Frequently asked questions

Does the tool count words in other languages?

Yes — it splits on whitespace and punctuation, so non-Latin scripts like Chinese, Japanese and Arabic count correctly.
