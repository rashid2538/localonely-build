About Fraction Calculator

Calculate with fractions — add, subtract, multiply or divide any two values, including mixed numbers and decimals. Every result is reduced to lowest terms and shown as a mixed number, an improper fraction and a decimal.

How to use Fraction Calculator

  1. Enter two fractions — 3/4, 2 1/3, 5 or 1.5 all work.
  2. Pick an operation.
  3. Read the reduced result: mixed, simple and decimal.

Supported formats & options

  • Input: two fractions (simple, mixed, whole or decimal)
  • Output: reduced fraction (mixed + simple) and decimal

Common use cases

  • Baking and cooking measurements
  • School maths homework and checking
  • Woodworking and construction measurements

Frequently asked questions

Can I enter mixed numbers?

Yes — type the whole number, a space, then the fraction, like "2 1/3". Decimals and whole numbers are accepted too.

Are results reduced?

Always to lowest terms, and the sign is normalized so a negative denominator is moved to the numerator.

What happens if I divide by zero?

A clear error is shown instead of an invalid result.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No — all the maths happens in your browser.
