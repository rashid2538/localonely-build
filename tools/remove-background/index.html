Remove Background

Remove the background from any photo with on-device AI. Free background remover that runs 100% in your browser — your image is never uploaded.

🔒 Your files never leave your device

About Remove Background

Upload a photo and the on-device AI model separates the subject from the background automatically, giving you a clean transparent PNG. A small matting model downloads once (≈12 MB, cached afterwards) and every cutout then runs locally in your browser via WebAssembly.

How to use Remove Background

  1. Choose a photo — it works best on people, portraits and clear silhouettes.
  2. Click Remove background; the first use downloads the small AI model.
  3. Fine-tune the edge cutoff or invert the mask, then download as PNG, WebP or JPEG.

Supported formats & options

  • Input: one image (PNG, JPEG, WebP, …)
  • Output: transparent PNG / WebP, or white-filled JPEG

Common use cases

  • Making a transparent product or profile picture
  • Replacing a busy background behind a person
  • Preparing images for cards, posters or collages

Frequently asked questions

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. The AI model runs on your device via WebAssembly; the only download is the small model file itself on first use, cached by your browser afterwards.

Which photos work best?

People, portraits and objects with clear silhouettes. Photos where the subject blends into the background, like hair against similar tones, may need the edge-cutoff slider to clean up.

Why JPEG instead of PNG?

JPEG has no transparency, so the cutout is filled with white. Choose PNG or WebP to keep a real transparent background.

Does it work offline?

Yes — after the first model download, all cutouts run locally with no connection at all.
